Hole #13 "Snaky Canyons"

Tips

The first of 2 par-5s in a row. Nice! You don't see that too often. #13 is challenging, what with navigating alongside and in between 2 canyons, but a birdie is not off the table. The steep treed-canyon that may have plagued you on #12 is still there, so there is NO forgiveness on the right. However, the very inviting 70-foot drop-off down to the fairway alleviates any canyon angst. On the left, a steep embankment catches drives that aren't too errant and happily and generously throws the ball down to the fairway, but watch out! Sometimes the ball rolls clear across the fairway into the canyon! The landing zone for most players' tee shot is somewhere between where the cart path crosses the fairway, and where the cart path later turns off to the right.

But wait! You might find a hidden tee box behind you. For a special challenge, you could tee off there.

Fairway and Approach Shot Info

For your wood shot, there's a trick you might want to know about! Yes, you could try and send the ball straight towards the green, but unless you're a long-hitter and/or had an exceptional drive, you may find it hard to clear the canyon on the left -- and keep the ball on the green. For more "normal" players, whack the ball more or less over the 150-yard marker towards the embankment on the right (but not over!), and be very pleasantly surprised at where your ball ends up. We won't tell you here, we'll let you find out for yourself! The approach shot can be tricky: you'll pretty much need a straight up and down, as balls like to roll off the back of the green into a hard-to-chip-out-of area. On top of that, the green is not very large.

Trivia: The tee box here might look like it could be the highest point on the course, and it's close, but no cigar: that honor belongs to the #15 green.

Green Info

If you're on the green, you're pretty much home free. Nothing notable here.
