Hole #10
Fairway and Approach Shot Info Green Info

Course Yardage

Black 390 yards
Blue 355 yards
White 317 yards
Red 293 yards

A very deep lateral ditch will not be a problem for most players. The green is a slight dogleg to the right, hidden behind a stand of tall cottonwoods. Landing the ball anywhere around the 150-yard marker is a reasonable drive. A little further and to the right sure won't hurt. Any shot towards the trees will most likely be a bad lie. A hook or bad pull will probably mean your ball is lost. The driving range is on the right, so don't use a yellow ball!

Fairway and Approach Shot Info

Unless the lie is bad, there's nothing special about the approach shot. There is a forgiveness all around the green.

Trivia: This fairway is the lowest part of the golf course.

Green Info

Most players will find this to be an easy green.
