Hole #5 "Old Man West" or "The Cottonwoods"
Fairway and Approach Shot Info Green Info

Hole #5

Hole Yardage

Black: 325 yards
Blue: 310 yards
White: 277 yards
Red: 228 yards
Marker: 100 yards
n

Tips

This very attractive dog-leg left hole drops some 35 feet to the fairway. The fairway is wide, but going left or right might result in a lost ball. This hole isn't called "The Cottonwoods" for nothing! You'd best use a non-white ball, as each cottonwood leaf can look like a white ball. Don't be fooled by the relatively short yardages: the green is pretty much hidden behind those cottonwoods. For some unknown reason, a lot of tee shots like to hook into the trees or the rough in front of the trees, so watch your aim. A good tee shot would be anywhere around the 100-yrd marker.

Tee Box Views

Fairway and Approach Shot Info

If your tee shot went too far past the 100-yd marker, you'll need a clean up-and-down to go over 2 front-side bunkers. Rolling off the green will mean a lost ball or a nice uphill chip shot. The green is otherwise not a tough challenge.

Trivia: Hey! Where is the black tee box?!? For fun, go tee off there for an exciting shot, but careful! To clear all those trees, you'll need a lot of carry! Some say 170 yards of carry, others say you'll need 190 yards of carry. You might send a spotter down there, because you'll be shooting blind!

Green Info

Once you're on the green, you shouldn't have too much trouble, but don't throw caution to the wind! The green is still sneaky enough to make you wonder why that ball broke left or right, but otherwise it's quite a playable green.
