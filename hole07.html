Hole #7

Hole Yardage

Black: 175 yards
Blue: 168 yards
White: 145 yards
Red: 136 yards

Tips

A nice view of Blue Mountain (over 11,300 ft high) may distract you from your shot!

While the flag is visible (the previous party did replace it, right?), the green is not; it’s 20 feet above you. A nice bank on the left provides generous forgiveness; many a golfer will find the ball nicely on the green after an otherwise seemingly errant shot, some even for a birdie tap-in! However, there is NO forgiveness on the right! The green-side bunkers on the right are your friends: they’ve saved many an otherwise lost ball. (DO NOT look for any lost balls in the canyon--seriously--unless you're a gymnast. It's not worth it. The footing is treacherous; it's not worth a broken leg to pick up a couple of balls!

Fairway and Approach Shot Info

Fell short of the green? No problem: a normal chip shot should set you straight. Don't go right!

Trivia: If it's afternoon, the sun could be shining straight into your eyes. Rather than go blind temporarily, it might be best to simply pick up the ball and go to the next hole.

Green Info

Most players will find this to be an easy green.
