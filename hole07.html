Hole #7

Hole Yardage

Black: 175 yards
Blue: 168 yards
White: 145 yards
Red: 136 yards

Tips

You're shooting uphill a fair bit, you can't see the green, but you can see the top of the flag (the previous party did replace it, right?) You'll want to send the ball straight or even left of the green (it'll bounce back), but any shot towards the right will bring your ball crashing (or rolling) into the trees and a deep canyon unless the 2 front-side green bunkers catch it. (DO NOT look for any lost balls there--seriously--unless you're a gymnast, you'll probably break a leg)

Tee Box Views

Fairway and Approach Shot Info

Fell short of the green? No problem: a normal chip shot should set you straight. Don't go right!

Trivia: If it's afternoon, the sun could be shining straight into your eyes. Rather than go blind temporarily, it might be best to simply pick up the ball and go to the next hole.

Green Info

Most players will find this to be an easy green.
