Hole #11 "The Funnel"
Fairway and Approach Shot Info Green Info

Hole #11

Hole Yardage

Black: 170 yards
Blue: 155 yards
White: 146 yards
Red: 96 yards

Tips

Before you tee off, take a minute to admire the elevated view. Look south to see #10, the driving range, the club house, and Monticello. Look West to see the Abajo Mountains again, look down, and you see #14 stretching out below you in front of your eyes ... and you're thinking: "Say, what if I teed off here for #14 ..." Hold that thought! Meanwhile, you're looking at a relatively narrow "funnel" to a very inviting green, which is tiered right and left. Thankfully, the green opens up right and left with some forgiveness on the left. A steep upwards slope behind the green saves long shots and 2 hidden bunkers to the right of the green save a lot of balls from almost certain death in the jungle. If you play it short, you're looking at a steep uphill chip shot. Watch out! The funnel part of the hole psyches out some golfers, so double check your aim before pulling the trigger.

Tee Box Views

Fairway and Approach Shot Info

There is no real fairway on this par-3, so if you're short, you're looking at a very steep uphill chip shot. You might not even be able to see the flag. If you have the misfortune of over-shooting the green, you're looking at a chip shot that's all downhill, including a green which in some places slopes back to front. So ... good luck with that!

Trivia: A powerful slice could put you on the green of the par-5 #13 ... for a possible tap-in for a double-eagle!

Green Info

This 2-tiered green can be tricky. Depending on where the ball lands, your putt might to send the ball into the apron before re-entering the green for a nice curve to the hold. Take some time to read the layout.
