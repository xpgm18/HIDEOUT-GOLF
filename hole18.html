Tips

This loooong and rather difficult par 4 is quite a pleasure to play. Starting at the tee boxes, the immediate surroundings are beautiful: nice grasses, shrubberies, and mature trees. Deer are often 150 or 200 yds down the fairway. Further down, a stream runs along the left side. Avoid going left, there's nothing but trouble over there; right isn’t a whole lot better, but does offer some ball-finding opportunities. The fairway is a bit narrow, although it widens in most players' landing zones. Thankfully, the fairway is downwards sloping, which is a great help.

Any shot placed near the 150-yd marker is a pretty reasonable place to be.

Don't forget that all-important rule of golf: "if this is your last hole, you have to be satisfied with your drive!" If not, try again, but watch out you don't hold up any party behind you.

Fairway and Approach Shot Info

The approach shot to the large green is not as long as it looks, although it can look intimidating with a pond short and right, and a stream running along the left side of the whole fairway and finally crossing from the left into the pond. The safer shot will bail out on the left.

Trivia: We hope you enjoyed your game! Passing thru? Do try and play again before you leave; playing just once leaves kind of a blur in the ol’ memory!

Green Info

Most players will find this to be a relatively easy green -- but don't let your guard down!
