Tips

Like most golf courses, a good opening hole, except this one starts off with a steep drop off! Nevertheless, this hole is not to be trifled with: to the right is lost-ball territory, so a slice and even a push is probably bad news. Watch your aim! The tee markers may not be pointing towards the green!

Tee Box Views

Fairway and Approach Shot Info

Unless the lie is bad, there's nothing special about the approach shot. There is forgiveness all around the green.

Green Info

Most players will find this to be an easy green.
