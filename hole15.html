Hole #15 "Face the Mountain"

Hole #15

Hole Yardage

Black: 292 yards
Blue: 280 yards
White: 239 yards
Red: 193 yards
Marker: 100 yards

Tips

An interesting hole that plays uphill initially before leveling out in a very slight dogleg left towards the green, which is not visible from the tee box. (Long hitters could have some fun here. Send a spotter down to the green!) There is considerable forgiveness on the right thanks to a steep embankment, but no forgiveness on the left. While this hole is rated “pretty easy”, many golfers find it can be pesky.

Pro-Amateur Tip: a reasonable drive is probably just past the 100-yd marker.

Tee Box Views

Fairway and Approach Shot Info

The green itself is guarded by a depression in front of it, so a bump-and-roll won't work. Sand bunkers guard left and right, but even worse, the green slopes down towards the back from the center with no forgiveness on the left. An up-and-down approach shot is probably best.

Green Info

Once you're on the green, you should be good to go.
