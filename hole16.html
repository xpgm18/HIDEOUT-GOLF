Hole #17 "Rising Above it All"
Fairway and Approach Shot Info Green Info

Hole #17

Hole Yardage

Black: 407 yards
Blue: 380 yards
White: 341 yards
Red: 315 yards
Marker: 150 yards

Tips

This is one of the more difficult holes on the course. While the fairway is nice and wide (thank goodness!), or at least it looks that way: a large part of the right fairway slopes down to the trees; many a golfer hits an otherwise very nice looking tee shot, only to find the ball bounced and rolled off course (typically from a bit of a slice)! From the tees it's all uphill to the green, some 40 feet above the tee box. There is no forgiveness on the left.

There is a depression with lots of “junk” and some trees right after the tee boxes, but will not present a problem for most players.

Tee Box Views

Fairway and Approach Shot Info

The green itself is accessible only via a relatively narrow gap in the tree line. Any approach shot that isn't straight will likely end up in the trees. Caution: Watch out for small cacti! An approach shot from a bit more on the right side of the fairway might present a better lie. The green is fairly large, but it's still possible to roll off the back of the green for a steep uphill chip shot.

Trivia: Bonus question: Can you find the black tee box?

The #16 green is the highest point on the whole course. Congratulations, you made it to the top!

Green Info

If you're on the green, it's very easy going. The hard part is behind you.
