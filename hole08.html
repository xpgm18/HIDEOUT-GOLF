Hole #8 "Raw Hide" or "The Diabolical One"
Fairway and Approach Shot Info Green Info

Hole #8

Hole Yardage

Black: 402 yards
Blue: 371 yards
White: 341 yards
Red: 268 yards
Marker: 150 yards

Tips

Pro/Amateur Tip: The wisest shot might be to layup in close proximity to the 150-yd marker, preferably to the right of it to avoid bouncing left.

You are now teeing off on a hole that has the dubious distinction of being what many say is the toughest hole on the course, although the 73-foot drop from tee-box to green is helpful.

A reasonably safe shot (if there is such a thing on this hole) is to simply take an iron and lay up close to the marker with either a straight shot, a fade, or a slight push-slice. Any ball going left or even bouncing left is almost certainly a lost ball as it careens down a steep slope towards a forested creek. A tendency to over-compensate by going right also results in a lost ball (if it crosses the cart path, otherwise it will roll nicely down to the fairway. Phew!)

Alternatively, take your driver and blast past the 150-yd marker down the steep hill--trouble is, you run the risk of the ball bouncing/rolling into the woods to the right or into the creek which traverses the fairway in front of the green, or you end up with a downhill lie. Whatever you choose to do -- good luck to you! You'll need it! ProTip #2: Want to have some fun? Try driving the green from the white or red tee-box.

Fairway and Approach Shot Info

Even if you had a good (or great!) tee shot, you're not out of the woods yet. This hole still has plenty of stuff left in its arsenal! For the approach shot, going too far will send the ball into the creek behind the green. Too short? You're in that creek again. There's nothing but cart path and woods to the left of the green, but strangely enough, few golfers go there. Right of the green there is plenty of forgiveness, but you're in the rough.

Trivia: Caution: There are STEEP inclines on this hole! Be very careful driving your golf cart. Do not tip over! It might be best to walk any steep inclines (these would be left of the fairway) rather than risk a bad accident. Be safe!

This hole enjoys the 2nd biggest drop on the course: 73 feet! (the #13 par 5 has a 74-foot drop)

Green Info

Phew! You finally made it to the green, so it's smooth sailing now. Ummm, except it's not. Remember the #6 green? Had some fun on that one, did we? Well, this #8 green is its twin sister - absolutely diabolical. Most players have a very hard time reading the green. Good luck, you're going to need it! You're on your own now.
