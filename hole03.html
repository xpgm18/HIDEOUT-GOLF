Hole #3 "Colorado Mountains"
Fairway and Approach Shot Info Green Info

Hole #3

Hole Yardage

Black: 156 yards
Blue: 140 yards
White: 125 yards
Red: 110 yards

Tips

An interesting, playable hole. You're shootin' into the Colorado mountains some 70 miles away! A shot that LOOKS like it's going too far right will bounce and roll on to the green, thanks to a steep right-to-left embankment between the green and the cart path. Going left? There is a certain amount of forgiveness left of the green, but don't push your luck!

Tee Box Views

Fairway and Approach Shot Info

If you're in the fairway, it looks like your tee shot fell short. A simple chip shot will probably work for most players.

Trivia: Take a minute and enjoy the beautiful view! After soaking in all that, turn around and look at the Abajo mountains right behind you. Yup, the're a lot closer aren't they? 11,300 feet high. You don't see this kind of stuff in, say, on a Big City course!

Green Info

This green is mostly sloping down towards the tee boxes. Some of the green feels like it slopes slightly left-to-right
