Tips

This classic American hole is fun to play. But wait! Remember the note up there on #11? It's right behind you. Why not go up there and tee off for an exciting elevated tee shot? Adds about 75 yards to your play.

The safe play for most folks is to drive towards the triple pine trees. A draw or even a bit of a pull-hook might be helpful to help shape the ball towards or around the slight dogleg left. The fairway bunker shouldn't be a problem: very few balls find their way there. It might be very tempting to take the shortcut over the edge of the trees on the left, but only if you're a long hitter: the trees don't open up as much as one might think. You'll need a carry somewhere in the neighborhood of 240+ yards. But what the hey! You only live once, so go for it!

Fairway and Approach Shot Info

The fairway is pretty wide, but long-hitters should take note of bunkers guarding the green. Otherwise, the fairway and approach shots are pretty much what they look like, unless you went too far to the right and trees are blocking your shot.

Trivia: A birdie is not off the table for this hole.

Green Info

A nice green. Unless your ball is in a weird place, there is nothing special about this green.
