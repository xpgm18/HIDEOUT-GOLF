Hole #6 "Quick Draw" or "Over the Treetops"
Fairway and Approach Shot Info Green Info

Hole #6

Hole Yardage

Black: 396 yards
Blue: 356 yards
White: 330 yards
Red: 288 yards
Marker: 150 yards

Tips

A beautiful hole with a wide fairway, slight dog-leg right. A marshy area right after the tee box adds to the allure, but won’t present a problem for most players. This is a nice, fun hole! For a sexy tee shot, shoot over the left part of the tall trees on the right. There's a slight dogleg right, but almost nothing but jungle on the right if you're short, so a draw or maybe even a bit of a pull-hook would be the safer shot if going over the trees. For most players, though, the safe tee shot is straight towards the 150-yd marker.

Fairway and Approach Shot Info

Well, if you tried out the "sexy" shot over the trees, and you're on the fairway, you might find your approach shot blocked by a small clump or two of fairway trees. But no worries--a pro like you will have no trouble going over those trees! For the rest of us who played the safe shot towards the 150-yd marker, we're facing an easy approach shot ... except maybe not so easy! Overshoot the green, or roll off the green, and the ball is almost certainly lost. There is, however, rich forgiveness on the right and for an added bonus, an approach shot that looks short and too far to the left will magically bounce and roll nicely on to the green. Not bad!

Trivia: A booming slice could put your ball on the green -- of #7! A tap-in, and you'll have bragging rights for an eagle, and nobody would be the wiser!

Green Info

Good news! (If you like a challenge!) This green, along wih the #8 green, is simply diabolical! Many players find it very difficult to read the green. Good luck -- you're on your own!
