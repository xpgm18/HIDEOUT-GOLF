Hole #17 "The Big Drop"
Fairway and Approach Shot Info Green Info

Hole #10

Hole Yardage

Black: 181 yards
Blue: 167 yards
White: 150 yards
Red: 136 yards

Tips

This hole may be the course favorite: a 67-foot drop down to the green! The green is generously sized; however, landing on the back of the green will slope your ball into no-man’s land. Watch out for a “cauldron” depression to the right of and in front of the green. Although the green is large, a hook shot will likely spell a lot of trouble.

Amateur-Tip: There is a lot of roughage between the tee boxes and the beginning of the token fairway. If you’re unsure of yourself, consider playing the red tees on this one.
Pro-Tip: Club down 3 clubs, at least 2. For example, if you’d normally use a 6 iron for the distance, try a 9 iron, or maybe an 8.

Tee Box Views

Fairway and Approach Shot Info

If you're chipping, the only thing to worry about is the back part of the green sloping down to the rear. If your chip shot runs away from you, your ball might find Mr. Creek.

Trivia: Wouldn't you just LOVE to have your house right here on this green? It's beautiful!

Green Info

Most players will find this to be an easy green.
