Hole #4

Hole Yardage

Black: 375 yards
Blue: 357 yards
White: 329 yards
Red: 305 yards
Marker: 150 yards

Tips

On your way to the distant #4 tee boxes, you saw the #17 tee box, didn’t you? And you wanted to stop and play it right now, didn’t you? Patience, you’ll get there!

This is a nice dog leg right with a valley between the marker and the green, which is not visible from the tee box. If you’re a long hitter and feeling very lucky, send a spotter down range and try your luck driving the green!

The more normal play is to lay up in the vicinity of the 150-yd marker. Some players like getting closer to the hole by shooting into the valley, but then you'll need a clean up-and-down towards a green that's not visible. Caution: a lot of errant tee shots like to go left or right, resulting in pretty much a lost ball.

Fairway and Approach Shot Info

This green is not known for having a lot of forgiveness. If you do miss the green, you'd best miss to the right of it.

Trivia: you're on the highest tee box of the course.

Green Info

It's a pretty straightforward green UNLESS the flag is in the front of the green: this part of the green slopes largely back-to-front. How rude!
