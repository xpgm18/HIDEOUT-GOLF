Hole #2 "Eye of the Needle"

Hole #2

Hole Yardage

Black: 558 yards
Blue: 500 yards
White: 475 yards
Red: 423 yards
Marker: 150 yards

Tips

Looks easy, right? It's not: this is one of the toughest tee shots on the course. For long hitters, there is a lateral stream in the "eye of the needle" that could come into play. For everybody else, the best tee shot might be a fade towards the center of the fairway. There is great risk of the ball landing and bouncing/rolling left. Your ball won't be lost, but the lie will be terrible. Going right means a wood shot over the trees, which is actually kind'a fun. If you send your ball into the rough/weeds to the right of the first trees you'll be facing a blind (but fun!) wood shot over thick forest, but the flag is right where you think it is, so go for it! Hey! If you can pull off a long, flying hook, you'll land on the green ... of #17! Do a tap in and you have bragging rights to a double-eagle ... as far as anybody else knows!

Fairway and Approach Shot Info

Your fairway wood shot is pretty much the way it looks: thread the needle and after that, any shot landing in the area of the 150-yd marker or after that is a pretty reasonable shot. Nothing tricky about the approach shot UNLESS the flag is behind the right green-side bunker. There isn't a lot of forgiveness on the right of the fairway, nor green, but most players should do fine on the approach shot. A short, but steep embankment behind the green will likely save approach shots that are too long.

Trivia: Bonus question: Can you find the black tee box? BTW, this hole is a great place to look for lost balls! But watch out you don't hold up anybody behind you.

Green Info

Most players will find this to be a relatively easy green if you're careful.
